Overview

    The file "mag-grav1vd.csv" holds magnetic and gravity survey data from a
    site near Cloncurry, east of Mount Isa, Queensland.

    Data source: Geoscience Australia Portal, by Geoscience Australia.

Fields

    Lon: Longitude (degrees east).
    Lat: Latitude (degrees north).
    Mag: Reduced to Pole magnetic map (nT).
    Grav_1vd: First vertical derivative of Bouguer gravity anomaly map (mGal/m).

Geographic Coordinate System

    Angular unit: 0.0174532925199433 degrees.

    Datum: Geocentric Datum of Australia 1994 (GDA94).
        Notes: Reference ellipsoid is GRS80 (Geodetic Reference System 1980).

    Longitude of prime meridian: 0.0 degrees.
        Notes: Angular distance offset from the standard Greenwich meridian.

    Semi-major axis: 6,378,137.0 metres.
        Notes: Equatorial radius, i.e., radius from the centre of the Earth to
               the equator.

    Inverse flattening: 298.257222101.
        Notes: Quantifies how much the Earth's sphere is flattened at the poles
               compared to the equator.
               Defined as a / (a - b)

               where,
                   a = semi-major axis,
                   b = semi-minor axis (polar radius, i.e. radius from the
                                        centre of the Earth to a pole).
